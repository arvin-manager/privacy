iOS private vault

Keep private files on this iPhone, encrypted and locked.

Privy is an encrypted Vault for photos, videos, and files. Your content stays on the device. There is no Privy account and no cloud copy of Vault files.

隱私庫是本機加密保險箱。照片、影片和檔案只存在這部 iPhone，可用 Face ID 或密碼鎖定。不必註冊帳號。

On-device AES-256 encryption Face ID or passcode lock No account required 本機加密 · 隱私庫

What Privy does

Encrypted Vault

Photos, videos, and documents are encrypted on the device with AES-256-GCM. Privy cannot see or recover your Vault content.

Lock and auto-lock

Unlock with Face ID, Touch ID, or a passcode. The Vault can lock immediately or after a short timer when you leave the app.

Private camera

Capture photos or video directly into the Vault. Camera access is used only when you choose to shoot or scan a QR code.

Safe Share

Export a privacy-protected copy when you choose. You can remove metadata and add a watermark before sharing.

Private browser

Browse over HTTPS in a non-persistent browser. Bookmarks can be saved in the Vault. Website data is cleared with the session.

Authorized downloads

Save a direct HTTPS file you own or have permission to keep. Privy does not bypass DRM, paywalls, or a website's access controls.

Advertising and purchases

The free app may show a Google AdMob banner. Ads are requested as non-personalized, contextual ads only. Privy does not use App Tracking Transparency or IDFA-based tracking across other apps.

Optional Vault Plus features are sold as an auto-renewing Apple subscription. Payment, cancellation, and refunds are handled by Apple through your Apple ID.
